EVERYTHING YOU
NEED TO FORM A TEAM

From discovery to daily collaboration — Mesh handles the whole journey.

Builder Profiles
Post your skills, track, timezone and what roles you're looking for. Get discovered by the right people building on Rialo.
Smart Invites
Send invites to builders you want to work with. They get notified instantly and can accept or decline right on the platform.
Team Chat
Once a team is formed, unlock a dedicated chat space for your squad. Real-time messaging between teammates.
Shared Draft Pad
A shared notes space inside every team chat. Write ideas, links, plans — both members can edit in real time.

Filter builders by track, role needed, and timezone. Find exactly the co-founder you're looking for in seconds.
